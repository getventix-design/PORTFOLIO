Not quite what you pictured?

Most of what leaves our counter is adjusted in some way: a lighter collar, a longer drop, a different stone, a motif copied from a grandmother's piece. Bring a photograph or a sketch and we will quote it by weight and making.

  • Weight adjusted for comfortable all-evening wear.
  • Stone type and colour chosen loose, under a loupe.
  • Family heirlooms re-set or restored where possible.
  • Timeline agreed in writing before work begins.
