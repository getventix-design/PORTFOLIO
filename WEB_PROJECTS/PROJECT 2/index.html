The House

A small house, a long apprenticeship

We are not a chain. Al-Kwait Jewellers is one showroom in Narowal, run by Asim Hussain and associated with AWF, where the person who shows you a piece is often the person who finished it.

That changes how jewellery gets made. Nothing leaves the bench until the karigar is satisfied with the back of the piece as much as the front. Weight, purity and making charges are written down before you decide — so the only surprise is how the gold looks in daylight.

Why families return

Four things we refuse to compromise

Open weighing

Gold is weighed in front of you. Purity, weight and making charges are stated separately, in writing, every time.

Made by hand

Drawing, filing, granulation and setting are done at the bench — not assembled from bought-in castings.

Stones you can inspect

Polki, uncut and brilliant-cut stones are shown loose under a loupe before they are set into your piece.

After-care for life

Cleaning, re-polishing, re-sizing and re-stringing for anything that leaves our counter.
